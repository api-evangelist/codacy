By Max Tatarchenko , CTO at SapientPro A software house finds out faster than most companies whether its rules for AI coding agents hold up. We run parallel teams across client codebases that share almost nothing, and the client owns every line at the end of an engagement. An agent that writes something careless inside one repository puts a contract at risk.
