A growing number of senior engineers describe a similar pattern: writing only a small amount of code manually while AI agents generate much of the rest. Imagine a senior engineer's day-to-day: they may write maybe twenty lines of code manually while AI agents generate the rest. Their job has shifted from typing implementation to defining intent, decomposing problems, and reviewing output.
